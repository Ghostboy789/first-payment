Winsorizing clips values to the pooled 1st and 99th percentiles (₹0.0 to 13,766.6 per week). Trimming drops rows outside those cutoffs. The trim’s weekly-profit interval crosses zero.

The missing-profit scenarios assign the cutoffs in opposite directions by arm. They bound the fitted coefficient for the clipped outcome under the stated support assumption. Each interval is conditional on its completed values and cutoffs.

Loan-use coverage

After excluding flagged records, the estimated change in business spending was +343.7 ₹/loan (95% CI −19.0 to 706.5 ₹/loan, n=786). The 95% interval fails the positive-effect check.

This follow-up check uses the released flag to select records and adjusts for batch and loan size. The provenance of the selected records is uncertain. See the loan-use source notes.
