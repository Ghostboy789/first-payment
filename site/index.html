Limits of the evidence

What this trial
cannot establish.

The grace period on its own
Maturity and effective interest changed with the repayment schedule.
Profit across all borrowers
Profit was measured only where it was recorded. Missing follow-up leaves the full-cohort effect unresolved.
A profit gain that holds after trimming
Trimmed profit was +335.0 ₹/week (95% CI −6.9 to 676.8 ₹/week, n=744). Profit gate FAIL.
