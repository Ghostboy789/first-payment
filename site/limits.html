Data and limits

What this trial can tell us.

The data cover 845 borrowers at one lender. Follow-up and contract terms set the limits of the decision.

Reading the estimates

A percentage point is the absolute difference between two rates. A 95% CI gives a range of estimates compatible with the data under the fitted model. Clustering accounts for shared outcomes among members of the same loan group.

The experiment compares assignment to whole contracts. The grace arm also had a longer maturity and a lower effective interest rate. Evidence comes from loans assigned in 2007 at one Kolkata lender. Applying it to another product or cohort would require new data.

Reported business profit is available for a subset of borrowers.

Loan-use survey coverage

The paper’s methods report 93% completion for the Survey 2 loan-use module. The Table 1 note says all clients completed it. The deposit confirms that the regression used 845 records with a value for business spending.

The Table 1 script adjusts for a flag that marks 59 records. Its regular-contract reference mean excludes those marked records. The 786 unflagged records are 93.0% of the file, which matches the reported completion rate after rounding. The deposit and appendix leave the flag’s meaning and survey provenance uncertain.
