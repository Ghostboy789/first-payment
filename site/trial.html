The trial

Two contracts, one randomized trial.

Village Financial Services assigned 169 loan groups in 9 batches. The experiment followed 845 borrowers in Kolkata.

What changed

Groups were formed and loans approved before assignment. The regular arm had 85 groups and the grace arm had 84. The grace contract also changed maturity and effective interest, even though nominal charges stayed the same.

Lender records tracked repayment. The loan-use survey measured how the loan was spent. The three-year survey asked about profits for current or most recently operating businesses.
