How to read it

Extra default clears its gate when the upper endpoint is within the cap. The profit gate requires the lower endpoint to be strictly above the floor.

The pilot starts at two extra defaults per 100 borrowers and credible positive profit. Changing the sliders tests a scenario. The original decision stays fixed.
