How to read it

Extra default clears its gate when the upper endpoint is at or below the cap. The profit gate requires the lower endpoint to be strictly above the floor.

The preset default cap was 2.00 percentage points and the preset profit floor was ₹0 per week. The controls model alternative thresholds for the same trial results.
